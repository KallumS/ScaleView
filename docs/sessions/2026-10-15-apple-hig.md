  question there (inverting it to the scheme's light button) is untouched.
- **10 points at least.** The floors were 7 and 9. A circle too small for a
  10-point name now shows none.
- **Keys for the menus.** S or Return, O.
- Pro first, then Simple, the same lines; the plugin follows.
